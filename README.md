## 👋🏼 Hello, anon friend

#### I am a [theoretical physicist by training](https://www.astro.sunysb.edu/steinkirch/)  (B.Sc. at University of Sao Paulo; M.Sc. *en route*, Ph.D. at Stony Brook University; graduate research at the Los Alamos National Laboratory and Brookhaven National Laboratory; [undergraduate research](https://web.archive.org/web/20090620090344/http://www.steinkirch.org/) at NASA Goddard Space Center) and a [principal software engineer by trade](https://marina.nullstar.fun/) (Shopify, Apple, Etsy, Yelp, startups, and private projects — both as an individual contributor and in leadership roles). Although I have deep expertise in several fields, I'm a proud generalist and systems thinker, and perhaps my greatest strengths lie in building fast, architecting creative solutions, and tackling new and challenging problems.

#### I started [coding in middle school](https://web.archive.org/web/20070322015644/http://fly.to/bytegirl) and during over nearly three decades of uninterrupted work in engineering and science, I've tackled many different problems. My story with AI/ML began back in high school, when I won first place in a national competition with an Expert Systems project (“AI Doctor”); continued during my Ph.D., when I wrote my thesis on the equation of state of neutron stars at Los Alamos (I also took graduate courses in ML and robotics during that time); and carried on through many [moments](https://singularity.nullstar.fun/ouroboros-statement.html) during my engineering career.
